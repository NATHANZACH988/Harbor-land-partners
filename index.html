THE HARBOR APPROACH

Land decisions start with better information.

Our work is buyer-first: we start with the geography, lot size, use case, and economics a buyer actually needs, then research the property and contact path. We focus on clear expectations, clean documentation, and realistic terms—not inflated estimates or promises.

01
Understand the property

Parcel identity, legal ownership, access, restrictions, and the seller's priorities.

02
Evaluate the fit

Buyer criteria, feasible use, comparable market evidence, and realistic pricing.

03
Discuss a practical next step

Clear communication about available options, transaction roles, and terms.
