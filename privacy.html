Privacy Notice

Draft for owner review · November 2026

Before launch: The business owner must review this notice and provide a verified business contact channel and final information-retention practices. This draft does not replace a jurisdiction-specific legal/privacy review.

Information we collect

If you submit an inquiry, we may receive the details you provide, including your name, email, phone number, property location, parcel identifier, asking price, property status, and any message or links you supply. Standard website hosting may also process basic technical data to operate and secure the site.

How the information is used

We use inquiry information to assess potential property or buyer-fit opportunities, respond to the person who submitted the inquiry, maintain an intake record, prevent spam or abuse, and administer the site. Information submitted through the site is not an offer or contract and does not create an agency relationship.

Service providers

Our website uses Netlify for site hosting and, when configured, form processing and notifications. If integrated by the site owner, inquiry records may also be routed to Airtable for internal review. These vendors process data under their respective service terms and policies.

Sharing and security

We do not publish submitted inquiries as listings. Inquiry details may be reviewed internally by authorized team members and with service providers necessary to handle the inquiry. Do not submit financial account credentials, identity documents, or other highly sensitive material through this form.

Your choices

You can ask about your submitted information or request that we stop contacting you by using our website inquiry form and clearly stating your request. Applicable privacy laws may provide additional rights.

Contact

Contact Harbor Land Partners through the website inquiry form, indicating “Privacy Request” in the details. A dedicated verified business contact address should be added before public launch.
